Canonical V2 runtime:
- server/webapp_v2_runtime.py

- V2 top-level functions: CORE / ORDERING / PI / PERFORMANCE.

Core data rules:
- 7D / 14D / 28D use completed canonical data-supported dates only.
- Missing source dates are ignored, never zero-filled.
- Family page shows Family 7D Sold and Family 28D Sold with supported-day count and UOM.
- All searches sort by 28D Qty Sold descending, then readiness.
- Historical SALE promotion cards show both dates and weekday range.
- Promotion = SALE only; TPR is not Promotion.

Ordering:
- CORE Ordering decision level is Family Article.
- Main Ordering scans FRESH only or FROZEN only.
- Eligibility: active selling in latest 28 supported completed POS dates AND DOS < 7.
- Negative Effective OH is displayed but ordering calculations use max(OH,0).
- Promotion items are selected by exact UPC and use that UPC's own historical SALE response.
- Final order suggestion remains Family Article level and covers through the day before next-next delivery.
- No Web App order save/submit workflow.

PI:
- PI target is always Family Article.
- Search may start from name / Family Article / Article / UPC.
- Family True Stock becomes authoritative Effective OH until superseded by a later accepted OH upload.
- Family PI supersedes active child-level True Stock for that family.
- No negative physical count.

Price:
- Price vs Sales chart shows latest 7 completed supported sales days.
- Price page also shows latest 14 completed supported days as detailed sales history.
- Historical promotion includes weekday range.

PERFORMANCE:
- Read-only performance report for ALL / LIVE / FRESH / FROZEN.
- Top 100 urgent price suggestions are analysis-only.
- Company Price C is a hard bottom line when available.
- Primary objective is higher observed GP$/day.
- Candidate demand response comes from actual historical supported-day sales at observed prices.
- Only positive GP$/day opportunities qualify for automatic suggestion.
- Active SALE and QA HOLD items are excluded from automatic price suggestions.

Mobile UI:
- Android/system Back navigates to the previous V2 screen instead of leaving the app flow.
- Elder-friendly presentation: larger type, larger tap targets, high contrast, clearer cards and simplified page hierarchy.
