Root cause addressed in this release:
- v7.0.36 considered any positive official cost with a non-null cost_uom_code safe
- actual repair additionally requires:
  1) authoritative Family linkage through existing Family, confirmed Article,
     source Article Number, or confirmed UPC identity
  2) cost_uom_code that resolves exactly to dim_uom

- therefore "non-null UOM" was too weak a SAFE criterion

  projected anchor, and repairable_by_current_rule
