v7.0.135 Upload Coverage Calendar:
- Upload page now includes a rolling calendar for the past 14 completed calendar days.
- Daily completeness uses the existing canonical FULL MYSQL daily coverage rule.
- A day is UPDATED only when both canonical POS Sales and MVT daily coverage are present.
- PARTIAL means one required daily source is present and the calendar explicitly shows whether POS or MVT is still needed.
- NEEDS UPLOAD means both required daily sources are missing.
- Price, OH, ABC, Article Manual and other periodic/non-daily sources do not determine daily calendar completeness.
- Calendar excludes the current incomplete business day and runs through the latest completed business date.
- Calendar refreshes when Upload opens, on manual Refresh, and when an upload batch reaches a terminal processing state.
- Current Batch and Recent Uploads continue to show per-file ingest status plus Phase2C, Phase3 and Quick Check READY/HOLD readiness.
